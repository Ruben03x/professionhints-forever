# Profession routes from 1 to 300

These are the recommendations currently bundled in [Routes.lua](../Routes.lua). The next row starts at the upper end of the preceding range. For example, **1–10** gives way to the next recommendation at 10. A route switch can come after a recipe becomes learnable. Crafting and gathering may take several attempts per skill point.

Both factions use the same leveling brackets and gathering zone lists. The tooltip shows up to three zones, ranked by distance when map data is available. Travel access, hostile areas, and access to recipe vendors can affect which option is practical.

## Rank caps and faction options

At a trained cap of 75, 150, or 225, the tooltip shows how to unlock the next rank. Where two destinations are listed below, the order is Alliance / Horde. A single destination applies to both factions. Mining, Herbalism, and Skinning offer several trainer choices per faction, ranked by proximity.

## Sources and updates

The [README lists the Forever references](../README.md#data-sources), including the Wowhead trainer pages used for Geofram Bouldertoe, Springspindle Fizzlegear, and Lilyssia Nightbreeze. The tables above describe the addon's current recommendations. The full set has yet to be retested in-game from 1 to 300.

When editing a route or trainer destination in [Routes.lua](../Routes.lua), update the matching table in the same change. Verify a recipe's learnable skill, source, and skill-up range when changing a breakpoint.
